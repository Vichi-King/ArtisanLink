ARTISANLINK SUPPORT

Help Center

Find quick answers to common questions about using ArtisanLink.

FOR CUSTOMERS
How do I find an artisan? +

Browse the available services from the homepage, choose the service you need, and explore artisans who offer that service.

How do I book an artisan? +

Open an artisan's profile, review their information and ratings, then follow the booking instructions to send a service request.

Can I review an artisan? +

Yes. After completing a service, customers can provide feedback through the platform's review system.

FOR ARTISANS
How do I become an artisan? +

Create an ArtisanLink account and select the option to become an artisan. You can then provide your professional information and services.

How do I manage my bookings? +

Accepted artisans can manage incoming service requests and their work through the artisan dashboard.

GENERAL
Is ArtisanLink free to use? +

Creating an account and discovering services on ArtisanLink is free. Any service charges or prices are determined according to the arrangement between customers and artisans.

How can I get additional assistance? +

For assistance beyond the information provided here, please contact the ArtisanLink support team.
