ARTISANLINK SUPPORT

Help Center

Find quick answers to common questions about using ArtisanLink.

FOR CUSTOMERS
How do I find an artisan?+

Browse available services, choose the service you need, and explore artisans who offer it.

How do I book an artisan?+

Open an artisan's profile, review their information and ratings, then choose a service and send a booking request.

Can I review an artisan?+

Yes. After the artisan marks the work complete, confirm completion and then submit your feedback.

FOR ARTISANS
How do I become an artisan?+

Create an ArtisanLink account and provide your professional information and services.

How do I manage my bookings?+

Use your artisan dashboard to manage incoming requests and update the progress of accepted work.

GENERAL
Is ArtisanLink free to use?+

Creating an account and discovering services is free. Service prices are set by artisans.

How can I get additional assistance?+

Please contact the ArtisanLink support team.
