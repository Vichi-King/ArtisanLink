ABOUT ARTISANLINK

Connecting people
with skilled hands.

ArtisanLink is a local service platform designed to make it easier for people to discover, connect with, and book trusted artisans for everyday jobs.

OUR STORY

Good work deserves to be found.

Finding a reliable artisan should not be difficult. Yet, many skilled professionals struggle to reach customers, while people searching for quality services often rely on word of mouth or uncertain recommendations.

ArtisanLink was created to bridge that gap. We provide a simple digital platform where customers can discover skilled local artisans, learn about their services, and connect with the right professional for the job.

From carpentry and electrical work to plumbing, painting, jewelry making, pottery and more, ArtisanLink brings local skills closer to the people who need them.
